Rett Syndrome Monitoring Biomarker Teaching Dataset

Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents a human longitudinal study in participants with Rett syndrome. The aim is to explore whether baseline physiological and psychophysical variables can predict broad future clinical state in a monitoring biomarker setting. Most measures are intentionally overlapping and weakly informative, with only a small amount of predictive structure.

Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed resting EEG, pupillometry during a visual orienting task, galvanic skin response recording during repeated sensory events, and brief psychophysical threshold procedures adapted for developmental ability. Caregivers also completed short ratings of autonomic variability and sensory burden. Follow-up visits were used to assign a two-level future clinical state.

Data Modalities & Variables

  • Age_Years — age at baseline in years
  • EEG_DeltaPower_uV2 — resting delta-band power in microvolt-squared units
  • EEG_PeakFrequency_Hz — dominant resting EEG peak frequency in hertz
  • Pupil_DilationPct — average task-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • GSR_Response_uS — mean galvanic skin response amplitude in microsiemens during stimulation blocks
  • Visual_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected reliably in percent
  • Tactile_Threshold_mN — tactile detection threshold in millinewtons
  • ReactionTime_ms — mean response time on the psychophysical task in milliseconds
  • Autonomic_Variability_Score — caregiver-rated autonomic variability score on a 0–100 scale
  • Sensory_Burden_Score — caregiver-rated sensory burden score on a 0–100 scale
  • Baseline_Clinical_State — baseline clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)
  • Future_Clinical_State — follow-up clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)

Groups

All participants in this dataset have Rett syndrome and were assessed at baseline and follow-up. The future clinical state is the intended target for monitoring biomarker exploration.
