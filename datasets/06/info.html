Rat Shank3B Partial Disruption Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal rat dataset from a preclinical study centered on a Shank3B partial disruption model of autism spectrum disorder.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

SHANK3-related models are widely used in neurodevelopmental research to study social communication changes and circuit-level variation relevant to autism spectrum disorder. This synthetic dataset represents a small translational study in adolescent rats combining behavioral, hormonal, electrophysiological, and simple physiological measurements. The main differences are concentrated in social-domain features, while spatial and general cognitive-style variables are broadly preserved across groups. The dataset is designed to feel plausible for early biomarker exploration without any single perfect separator.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Rats completed a compact test battery including social approach, social novelty preference, Y-maze alternation, and novel object location testing during the same assessment period. Resting neural and autonomic measures were collected alongside a plasma oxytocin assay and a general cognitive electrophysiology index. A later functional outcome category was assigned after short-term follow-up under standardized housing and monitoring conditions.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the rat (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized preference score for social interaction (unitless ratio)
  • Social_Novelty_Index — normalized preference score for a novel conspecific (unitless ratio)
  • Ultrasonic_Call_Rate_per_min — rate of social ultrasonic vocalizations during interaction (calls/min)
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • YMaze_Alternation_pct — spontaneous alternation rate in the Y-maze (%)
  • Novel_Object_Location_Index — preference index for the relocated object (unitless ratio)
  • PPI_pct — prepulse inhibition magnitude as a percentage
  • Plasma_Oxytocin_pg_mL — plasma oxytocin concentration (pg/mL)
  • Resting_Theta_Power_uV2 — resting theta-band EEG/LFP power (µV², arbitrary processed units)
  • Social_Evoked_HbO_AU — stimulus-evoked hemodynamic response during social cue presentation (arbitrary units)
  • Cognitive_P300_Latency_ms — latency of a simple cognitive event-related potential component (ms)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category at follow-up

5. Groups

Shank3B_Partial rats carry a partial Shank3B disruption used here as a model relevant to autism spectrum disorder. WT rats are wild-type controls assessed with the same procedures, timing, and recording methods.
