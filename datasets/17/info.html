Preclinical Biomarker Dataset: Autism Spectrum Disorder Mouse Model

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage preclinical biomarker study in a mouse model of autism spectrum disorder. The study combines physiological, psychophysical, and behavioral measures to mimic a translational screening battery used in exploratory biomarker discovery. Group separation is driven mainly by physiological and psychophysical variables, while behavioral measures remain strongly overlapping.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mice underwent resting EEG, pupillometry, sleep fragmentation monitoring, and galvanic-like arousal recording. They also completed psychophysical testing including auditory threshold detection, prepulse inhibition, visual contrast sensitivity, and tactile withdrawal threshold. Behavioral assays included social interaction, novelty exploration, and locomotor variability, but these measures were designed to show limited group discrimination.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or ASD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within the mouse cohort
  • EEG_ThetaGamma_Ratio — resting EEG theta-to-gamma power ratio
  • Pupil_Novelty_Dilation_pct — percent pupil dilation during novelty exposure
  • Sleep_Fragmentation_Index — normalized index of sleep interruption frequency
  • Arousal_Reactivity_Z — standardized physiological arousal response to challenge
  • Auditory_Threshold_dB — minimum sound level detected during psychophysical testing
  • Prepulse_Inhibition_pct — percent reduction of startle response after prepulse
  • Visual_Contrast_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected during testing
  • Tactile_Withdrawal_Threshold_g — force threshold for withdrawal response in grams
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized level of social engagement during the task
  • Novelty_Exploration_Time_s — seconds spent exploring a novel object or arena
  • Locomotor_Variability_SD — within-session variability of movement level
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mice without the disorder-model label in this study framework. The ASD group includes mice assigned to the autism spectrum disorder model group based on the study design.
