Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in panic disorder using human participants only. The study combines psychophysical threshold and sensory-response tasks with physiological recordings and several extra variables that are not expected to differ between groups. Group separation is carried mainly by psychophysical variables, while physiological measures remain broadly overlapping.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed breathlessness detection, interoceptive heartbeat counting, acoustic discomfort threshold, and contrast sensitivity testing. They also underwent resting EEG, heart-rate variability, skin conductance, pupillometry, and respiratory variability recording. Several additional variables were included as low-information background measures to mimic realistic exploratory datasets.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or PD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Breathlessness_Detection_Threshold — minimum inspiratory resistance level detected during psychophysical testing
  • Heartbeat_Counting_Error_pct — percent error during interoceptive heartbeat estimation
  • Acoustic_Discomfort_Threshold_dB — sound intensity rated as uncomfortable in decibels
  • Contrast_Detection_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected during testing
  • EEG_ThetaBeta_Ratio — resting frontal theta-to-beta power ratio
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • SkinConductance_Reactivity_Z — standardized electrodermal response amplitude
  • Pupil_Dilation_Challenge_pct — percent pupil dilation during mild challenge
  • Respiratory_Variability_SD — variability of breathing interval on a normalized scale
  • BlinkRate_per_min — spontaneous blink rate per minute during baseline recording
  • FingerTap_Variability_SD — variability of paced finger tapping during control task
  • VisualSearch_RT_ms — response time during simple visual search in milliseconds
  • RandomQuestionnaire_Index — composite low-information questionnaire summary on a normalized scale
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without panic disorder in the study framework. The PD group includes human participants assigned to the panic disorder group based on the study design.
