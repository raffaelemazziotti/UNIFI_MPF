Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in obsessive-compulsive disorder using human participants only. The study combines psychophysical threshold and cognitive-control tasks with physiological recordings and several extra variables that are not expected to differ between groups. Group separation is carried mainly by physiological variables, while psychophysical measures remain broadly overlapping.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed sensory threshold and response-inhibition style psychophysical tasks together with resting EEG, autonomic recording, pupillometry, and skin conductance measurement. Additional low-information background variables were included to mimic realistic exploratory data tables in which several columns have little or no group relevance. The dataset is intentionally fuzzy and suitable for introductory biomarker discovery.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or OCD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Tactile_Detection_Threshold_g — force threshold for tactile detection in grams
  • TemporalOrder_JND_ms — just-noticeable difference for temporal order judgment in milliseconds
  • StopSignal_RT_ms — estimated stop-signal reaction time in milliseconds
  • PerceptualSwitch_Rate_per_min — number of perceptual switches per minute during bistable viewing
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_ErrorRelated_Negativity_AU — normalized error-related negativity amplitude
  • EEG_Coherence_FrontoStriatal — EEG coherence summary between frontal and striatal-related channels
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • SkinConductance_Reactivity_Z — standardized electrodermal response amplitude
  • Pupil_Dilation_Challenge_pct — percent pupil dilation during response conflict challenge
  • Respiratory_Variability_SD — variability of breathing interval on a normalized scale
  • SimpleRT_ms — response time during simple reaction-time task in milliseconds
  • ColorPreference_Index — normalized preference score from unrelated color-choice task
  • TypingSpeed_wpm — typing speed in words per minute during neutral task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without obsessive-compulsive disorder in the study framework. The OCD group includes human participants assigned to the obsessive-compulsive disorder group based on the study design.
