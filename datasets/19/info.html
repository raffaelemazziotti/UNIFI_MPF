Preclinical Connectivity Dataset: CDKL5 Deficiency Disorder Mouse Model

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage preclinical biomarker study in a mouse model of CDKL5 deficiency disorder. The study focuses on mesoscale and cellular connectivity readouts derived from intrinsic signal optical imaging and calcium imaging, together with a small set of behavioral measures. The dataset is intentionally noisy and overlapping so that connectivity patterns are suggestive rather than definitive.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mice underwent resting-state intrinsic signal optical imaging to estimate large-scale functional connectivity between cortical regions, followed by calcium imaging to quantify network coordination and synchrony during spontaneous activity. Behavioral testing included simple social interaction, locomotor exploration, and sensorimotor performance tasks. Measures were summarized as interpretable connectivity features suitable for introductory biomarker discovery and classification exercises.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or CDD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within the mouse cohort
  • ISOI_FrontoParietal_FC — intrinsic signal optical imaging functional connectivity between frontal and parietal cortex (correlation scale)
  • ISOI_Interhemispheric_Somatomotor_FC — intrinsic signal optical imaging interhemispheric somatomotor connectivity (correlation scale)
  • ISOI_DefaultLike_Modularity — modularity estimate of the cortical intrinsic signal network
  • ISOI_SensoryAssociation_Coupling — intrinsic signal coupling between sensory and association regions (correlation scale)
  • Ca_PrefrontalEnsemble_Synchrony — calcium imaging synchrony across prefrontal neuronal ensembles (normalized index)
  • Ca_Somatomotor_Coactivity_Rate — rate of coactive calcium events in somatomotor cortex (events/min)
  • Ca_LongRange_Correlation — long-range calcium activity correlation across imaged regions
  • Ca_Network_Burst_Probability — probability of large-scale calcium network bursts
  • Ca_SeededConnectivity_V1_PFC — seeded calcium connectivity between visual cortex and prefrontal cortex
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized level of social engagement during the task
  • OpenField_Distance_m — distance traveled during open-field exploration in meters
  • Rotarod_Latency_s — latency to fall during motor coordination testing in seconds
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mice without the disorder-model label in this study framework. The CDD group includes mice assigned to the CDKL5 deficiency disorder model group based on the study design.
