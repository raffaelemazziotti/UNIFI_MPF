Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Generalized Anxiety Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Generalized Anxiety Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in generalized anxiety disorder using human participants. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, autonomic measures, and blood-based markers, together with a few low-information variables that do not meaningfully separate the groups. The overall structure is intentionally overlapping, with only modest shifts across a subset of variables.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical assessment session and a sustained worry-induction task. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and autonomic measures were collected during the visit, and morning blood samples were processed for endocrine and inflammatory summaries. The resulting variables were organized into a single table for exploratory classification and prediction of future outcome category.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or GAD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Anxiety_Score — continuous clinical anxiety severity score
  • Clinical_Worry_Interference_Score — continuous score for daily-life interference from worry
  • Clinical_Sleep_Complaint_Score — continuous clinical sleep complaint score
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_AlphaPeak_Hz — peak alpha frequency in hertz
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during worry induction in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_LeftRight_Coupling — coupling summary between bilateral frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • CRP_mg_L — C-reactive protein concentration in milligrams per liter
  • Pupil_Dilation_Challenge_pct — percent pupil dilation during mild challenge
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — normalized score from unrelated color preference task
  • Random_TypingSpeed_wpm — typing speed during neutral task in words per minute
  • Random_NumberEstimation_Error — absolute error on unrelated number estimation task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without generalized anxiety disorder in the study framework. The GAD group includes human participants assigned to the generalized anxiety disorder group based on the study design.
