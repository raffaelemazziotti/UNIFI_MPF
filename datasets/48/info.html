Synthetic teaching dataset

Creatine Transporter Deficiency Monitoring Biomarker Teaching Dataset

Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents a human longitudinal study in children with creatine transporter deficiency. The study is structured for early monitoring biomarker teaching, where baseline physiological and psychophysical measures have only a very small relation to later prognosis. The variables were built to remain strongly overlapping across outcome levels.

Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed resting EEG, pupillometry during an oddball paradigm, galvanic skin response recording during repeated sensory events, and brief psychophysical threshold tasks adapted for developmental ability. Caregivers also completed short ratings of fatigue and everyday adaptive behavior. Follow-up visits were used to define a two-level prognosis category based on overall clinical course.

Data Modalities & Variables

  • Age_Years — age at baseline in years
  • EEG_ThetaPower_uV2 — resting theta-band power in microvolt-squared units
  • EEG_P300Latency_ms — auditory oddball P300 latency in milliseconds
  • Pupil_DilationPct — average task-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • GSR_Response_uS — mean galvanic skin response amplitude in microsiemens during stimulation blocks
  • Auditory_Threshold_dB — auditory detection threshold in decibels
  • Temporal_Order_Threshold_ms — minimum temporal order interval detected reliably in milliseconds
  • ReactionTime_ms — mean response time on the psychophysical task in milliseconds
  • Fatigue_Score — caregiver-rated fatigue score on a 0–100 scale
  • Adaptive_Function_Score — caregiver-rated adaptive function score on a 0–100 scale
  • Baseline_Clinical_State — baseline clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)
  • Future_Prognosis — follow-up prognosis category (Stable or Worsened)

Groups

All participants in this dataset have creatine transporter deficiency and were assessed at baseline and follow-up. The future prognosis variable is the intended monitoring outcome.
