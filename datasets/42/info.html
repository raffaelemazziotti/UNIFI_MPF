Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Social Anxiety Disorder Patient Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Social Anxiety Disorder Patient Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage longitudinal biomarker study in patients with social anxiety disorder. All subjects belong to the same baseline patient cohort, and the main aim is to predict later disease state from a compact baseline profile. Only a small subset of variables is mildly useful, while the remaining variables are weak or random with respect to the follow-up target.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Patients completed baseline clinical assessment, resting EEG, frontal fNIRS during a social-evaluation task, blood pressure and galvanic skin response recording, and a simple reaction-time measure. Follow-up reassessment classified each patient into one of two later disease states and also recorded binary treatment response. The dataset is intentionally small and fuzzy so that no single variable solves the task cleanly.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — baseline cohort label for all subjects (SAD_Patient)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the patient cohort
  • Clinical_SocialFear_Severity — baseline continuous social fear severity score
  • Clinical_Avoidance_Impact — baseline continuous avoidance-related functional impact score
  • Clinical_Anticipatory_Anxiety — baseline continuous anticipatory anxiety score
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during social evaluation in micromolar units
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_SocialChallenge_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during social challenge
  • SocialCue_RT_ms — response time during social cue task in milliseconds
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — unrelated normalized color preference score
  • Random_BlinkRate_per_min — unrelated spontaneous blink rate during baseline
  • Treatment_Response — binary treatment response category at follow-up
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later disease state category at follow-up

5. Groups

All subjects belong to the same baseline social anxiety disorder patient cohort. At follow-up, patients can fall into one of two later clinical state categories.
