ADHD Monitoring Biomarker Teaching Dataset

Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents a human longitudinal study in children with ADHD. It is designed to mimic a frustrating monitoring biomarker scenario in which baseline variables show almost no consistent relationship to future prognosis. Most measures are noisy, overlapping, and effectively unrelated to later status.

Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed resting EEG, pupillometry during a sustained attention task, galvanic skin response recording during repeated cues, and simple psychophysical timing procedures. Caregivers also completed brief ratings of regulation and fatigue. Follow-up visits were used to assign a broad two-level prognosis category.

Data Modalities & Variables

  • Age_Years — age at baseline in years
  • EEG_ThetaBetaRatio — resting EEG theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_P300Latency_ms — auditory oddball P300 latency in milliseconds
  • Pupil_DilationPct — average task-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • GSR_Response_uS — mean galvanic skin response amplitude in microsiemens during cue blocks
  • Temporal_Discrimination_ms — threshold for temporal discrimination in milliseconds
  • Motion_Coherence_Threshold_pct — minimum motion coherence detected reliably in percent
  • ReactionTime_ms — mean response time on the psychophysical task in milliseconds
  • Daily_Regulation_Score — caregiver-rated daily regulation score on a 0–100 scale
  • Fatigue_Behavior_Score — caregiver-rated fatigue behavior score on a 0–100 scale
  • Baseline_Clinical_State — baseline clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)
  • Future_Prognosis — follow-up prognosis category (Stable or Worsened)

Groups

All participants in this dataset have ADHD and were assessed at baseline and follow-up. The future prognosis variable is intended as the monitoring target rather than a group label.
