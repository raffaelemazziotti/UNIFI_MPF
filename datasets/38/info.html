Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in bipolar disorder using human participants only. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, galvanic skin response, reaction-time summaries, and threshold measures, together with several unrelated random variables. Clinical variables show the strongest group-related shifts, whereas physiological and task-based measures are weaker and more heavily overlapped.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical evaluation session together with reward anticipation, emotional conflict, and sensory threshold tasks. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and galvanic skin response were measured during the visit, and reaction-time and psychophysical threshold summaries were extracted from simple laboratory tasks. A few unrelated variables were included to mimic realistic noisy exploratory datasets.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or BD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Mood_Instability_Score — continuous clinical mood instability score
  • Clinical_Energy_Dysregulation_Score — continuous score for energy and activation dysregulation
  • Clinical_Impulsivity_Score — continuous clinical impulsivity score
  • Clinical_SleepRhythm_Disruption_Score — continuous score for sleep and circadian disruption
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_GammaPower_Relative — relative resting gamma-band power
  • fNIRS_DLPFC_HbO_Change_uM — dorsolateral prefrontal oxyhemoglobin change during reward condition in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Frontal_Coupling — coupling summary across frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_RewardConflict_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during reward conflict
  • RewardBias_RT_ms — response time bias during reward anticipation in milliseconds
  • Visual_Contrast_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected during testing
  • Auditory_Discomfort_Threshold_dB — sound intensity rated as uncomfortable in decibels
  • Random_PatternPreference_Index — normalized score from unrelated pattern preference task
  • Random_DeskTapping_Rate — tapping count during neutral desk task
  • Random_WordPuzzle_Score — score from unrelated word puzzle task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without bipolar disorder in the study framework. The BD group includes human participants assigned to the bipolar disorder group based on the study design.
