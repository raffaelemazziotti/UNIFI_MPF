Clinical Connectivity Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Connectivity Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in bipolar disorder using human participants only. The study combines behavioral task summaries, EEG-derived features, autonomic physiology, and simple connectivity measures to create a small multimodal dataset for exploratory analysis. The data are intentionally fuzzy and overlapping, with only a few variables showing modest group shifts.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed an emotional face attention task, probabilistic reversal learning, a delayed working memory task, and a simple social reward preference measure. Resting EEG and heart-rate variability were recorded, and network summaries were extracted from frontolimbic and interhemispheric connectivity estimates. The resulting features are suitable for introductory group classification and exploratory follow-up outcome modeling.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or BD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • EmotionAttention_Bias_ms — response time bias toward emotional faces in milliseconds
  • ReversalLearning_Errors — number of errors after contingency reversal
  • WorkingMemory_Accuracy_pct — percent correct on delayed working memory trials
  • SocialReward_Preference_Index — normalized preference for social reward over neutral option
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_LeftRight_Alpha_Asymmetry — normalized frontal alpha asymmetry index
  • EEG_Coherence_FrontoLimbic — EEG coherence summary between frontal and limbic-related channels
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Pupil_Reactivity_pct — percent pupil change during affective challenge
  • Conn_FrontoAmygdala_FC — functional connectivity summary between frontal and amygdala-related regions
  • Conn_Interhemispheric_Prefrontal_FC — connectivity summary between bilateral prefrontal regions
  • Movement_Variability_SD — within-task variability of movement or response level
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without bipolar disorder in the study framework. The BD group includes human participants assigned to the bipolar disorder group based on the study design.
