Mouse Syngap1 Haploinsufficiency Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal mouse dataset from a preclinical study centered on a Syngap1 haploinsufficiency model of SYNGAP1-related neurodevelopmental disorder.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

SYNGAP1-related neurodevelopmental disorder has been modeled in mice to examine variation in sensory processing, arousal regulation, cognitive function, and social behavior. This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage biomarker-style experiment with mixed behavioral and physiological readouts collected in young adult mice. Group differences are present but intentionally overlapping, so many subjects appear only mildly shifted rather than clearly separated. The dataset is designed for introductory classification and prediction tasks in a realistic, noisy setting.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Mice completed a compact battery including open-field exploration, social interaction, novel object discrimination, and a simple sensory-gating assay. Resting neural activity, auditory-evoked responses, and autonomic reactivity were recorded during the same testing period. A later binary functional outcome label was assigned after short standardized follow-up observation under stable housing conditions.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the mouse (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • Center_Time_pct — percentage of open-field time spent in the center zone
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized preference score for social interaction (unitless ratio)
  • Novel_Object_Discrimination_Index — preference index for the novel object (unitless ratio)
  • PPI_pct — prepulse inhibition magnitude as a percentage
  • Resting_Gamma_Power_uV2 — resting gamma-band EEG/LFP power (µV², arbitrary processed units)
  • Auditory_N1_Amplitude_uV — amplitude of an auditory-evoked negative component (µV)
  • Cortical_Connectivity_Index — summary measure of large-scale cortical coupling (unitless index)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Locomotor_Habituation_Slope — within-session reduction in locomotion across time bins (unitless slope)
  • Future_Outcome_Binary — later functional outcome category at follow-up (Resilient or Vulnerable)

5. Groups

Syngap1_Het mice carry a heterozygous Syngap1 alteration used here as a model relevant to SYNGAP1-related neurodevelopmental disorder. WT mice are wild-type controls assessed with the same procedures and recording methods.

6. Research Tasks

Classify subjects into groups using the multimodal physiological and behavioral variables.

Predict future outcome category from the baseline measurements.
