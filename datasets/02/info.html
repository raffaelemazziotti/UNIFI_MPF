Rat Slc6a8 Deficiency Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal rat dataset from a preclinical study centered on an Slc6a8 deficiency model of creatine transporter deficiency.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

Creatine transporter deficiency is a neurodevelopmental disorder associated with cognitive and behavioral difficulties and has been modeled in rodents using Slc6a8-related manipulations. This synthetic dataset represents a small translational study in young rats combining physiological, neural, and behavioral measures. The data are designed to resemble an early biomarker screen with substantial heterogeneity across subjects. Some features carry mild group structure, but no single measure cleanly separates the groups.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Young rats completed a compact test battery including open-field exploration, novel object recognition, social interaction, and a simple maze assay. Resting neural activity, sensory gating, arousal-related responses, and movement-linked physiological measures were collected during the same testing period. A later functional outcome category was assigned after short-term follow-up under standardized housing and monitoring conditions.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the rat (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • Center_Time_pct — percentage of open-field time spent in the center zone
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized social preference score (unitless ratio)
  • Novel_Object_Discrimination_Index — preference index for the novel object (unitless ratio)
  • YMaze_Alternation_pct — spontaneous alternation rate in the Y-maze (%)
  • Grip_Strength_N — forelimb grip strength (newtons)
  • PPI_pct — prepulse inhibition magnitude as a percentage
  • Resting_Theta_Power_uV2 — resting theta-band EEG/LFP power (µV², arbitrary processed units)
  • Hippocampal_Creatine_Signal_AU — normalized hippocampal creatine-related signal from imaging/spectroscopy style readout (arbitrary units)
  • Locomotor_Habituation_Slope — within-session reduction in locomotion across time bins (unitless slope)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category at follow-up

5. Groups

Slc6a8_Def rats model creatine transporter deficiency through reduced Slc6a8 function. WT rats are wild-type controls assessed with the same procedures and recording methods.

6. Research Tasks

Classify subjects into groups using the multimodal physiological and behavioral variables.

Predict future outcome category from the baseline measurements.
