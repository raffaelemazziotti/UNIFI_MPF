Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Social Anxiety Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Social Anxiety Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in social anxiety disorder using human participants only. The dataset combines social-behavioral task measures, eye tracking, autonomic reactivity, and EEG summaries to create an introductory multimodal dataset with partial but imperfect group structure. The measures were designed to remain substantially overlapping, with only a few variables showing modest shifts.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a gaze-to-face task, a public-speaking anticipation challenge, a simple emotion recognition task, and a social approach-avoidance decision task. Eye tracking, galvanic skin response, pupillometry, and resting EEG were collected, along with simple connectivity summaries reflecting frontoparietal and salience-network communication. The resulting feature space is suitable for exploratory classification and follow-up outcome prediction.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or SAD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • FaceGaze_DwellTime_ms — average dwell time on faces during social viewing in milliseconds
  • SpeechAnticipation_GSR_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during speech anticipation
  • EmotionRecognition_Accuracy_pct — percent correct on emotion recognition trials
  • ApproachAvoidance_Bias_ms — reaction-time bias for avoiding social stimuli in milliseconds
  • Pupil_SocialCue_Dilation_pct — percent pupil dilation during social cue exposure
  • EEG_Posterior_AlphaPower_Relative — relative posterior alpha-band power
  • EEG_ThetaSynchrony_FrontoParietal — theta synchrony summary between frontal and parietal regions
  • Resting_HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Conn_SalienceNetwork_FC — functional connectivity summary within salience-related regions
  • Conn_FrontoParietal_FC — functional connectivity summary within frontoparietal regions
  • BlinkRate_per_min — spontaneous blink rate per minute during baseline recording
  • Movement_Variability_SD — within-task variability of movement or response level
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without social anxiety disorder in the study framework. The SAD group includes human participants assigned to the social anxiety disorder group based on the study design.
