Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Tourette Syndrome Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Tourette Syndrome Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in Tourette syndrome using human participants. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, autonomic measures, and blood-based markers, along with several random low-information variables that do not show meaningful group separation. The overall pattern is intentionally fuzzy, with modest differences carried by only part of the feature space.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical evaluation session and a motor inhibition challenge. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and autonomic measures were obtained during the visit, and morning blood samples were collected for endocrine and inflammatory summaries. Continuous clinical variables and physiological summaries were combined into one dataset for exploratory classification and future outcome prediction.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or TS)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Tic_Severity_Score — continuous clinical tic severity score
  • Clinical_Urge_Intensity_Score — continuous score for premonitory urge intensity
  • Clinical_Functional_Impact_Score — continuous score for daily functional impact
  • EEG_Sensorimotor_BetaPower_Relative — relative beta-band power over sensorimotor regions
  • EEG_FrontoCentral_Coherence — coherence summary across frontocentral EEG channels
  • fNIRS_SMA_HbO_Change_uM — supplementary motor area oxyhemoglobin change during inhibition challenge in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_FrontalMotor_Coupling — coupling summary between frontal and motor fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • Ferritin_ng_mL — ferritin concentration in nanograms per milliliter
  • Pupil_Dilation_Challenge_pct — percent pupil dilation during response suppression challenge
  • Random_PatternChoice_Index — normalized score from unrelated pattern preference task
  • Random_DeskTapping_Rate — tapping count during neutral desk task
  • Random_TonePreference_Score — score from unrelated tone pleasantness ratings
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without Tourette syndrome in the study framework. The TS group includes human participants assigned to the Tourette syndrome group based on the study design.
