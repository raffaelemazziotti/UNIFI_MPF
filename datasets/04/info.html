Rat Fmr1 Knockdown Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal rat dataset from a preclinical study centered on an Fmr1 knockdown model of fragile X syndrome.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

Fragile X syndrome is a neurodevelopmental disorder linked to disruption of FMR1-related pathways and has been modeled in rodents using partial or full reduction of Fmr1 function. This synthetic dataset represents a compact preclinical biomarker-style study in adolescent rats combining behavioral testing with neural and autonomic readouts. The behavioral profiles are intentionally broad and overlapping, while the physiological features show stronger but still imperfect group structure. The dataset is designed for introductory multimodal classification and prediction exercises.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Rats completed open-field, social interaction, novel object discrimination, and rotarod testing during a single assessment window. Resting neural synchrony, sensory-gating, autonomic reactivity, and a simple optical-imaging-style signal were collected in parallel. A later functional outcome category was assigned after standardized follow-up monitoring under stable housing conditions.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the rat (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • Center_Time_pct — percentage of open-field time spent in the center zone
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized social preference score (unitless ratio)
  • Rotarod_Latency_s — latency to fall from the accelerating rotarod (seconds)
  • Novel_Object_Discrimination_Index — preference index for the novel object (unitless ratio)
  • PPI_pct — prepulse inhibition magnitude as a percentage
  • Resting_Gamma_Power_uV2 — resting gamma-band EEG/LFP power (µV², arbitrary processed units)
  • Cortical_Connectivity_Index — summary measure of large-scale cortical coupling (unitless index)
  • Stimulus_Evoked_HbO_AU — stimulus-evoked oxygenated hemoglobin response from imaging-style recording (arbitrary units)
  • Respiratory_Variability_Index — normalized breath-to-breath variability index (unitless)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category at follow-up

5. Groups

Fmr1_KD rats show reduced Fmr1 function and are used here as a model relevant to fragile X syndrome. WT rats are wild-type controls assessed with the same procedures, timing, and recording methods.

6. Research Tasks

Classify subjects into groups using the multimodal physiological and behavioral variables.

Predict future outcome category from the baseline measurements.
