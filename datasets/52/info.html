Tourette Disorder Monitoring Biomarker Teaching Dataset

Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents a human longitudinal study in children and adolescents with Tourette disorder. The purpose is to explore whether baseline physiological and psychophysical measurements can predict broad future clinical state in a monitoring biomarker setting. Most variables are intentionally weak, noisy, and largely unrelated to later prognosis.

Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed resting EEG, pupillometry during a simple inhibitory control task, galvanic skin response recording during repeated cue blocks, and brief psychophysical timing and tactile threshold procedures. Caregivers also completed short ratings of arousal and everyday sensory regulation. Follow-up visits were used to assign a two-level future clinical state.

Data Modalities & Variables

  • Age_Years — age at baseline in years
  • EEG_BetaPower_uV2 — resting beta-band power in microvolt-squared units
  • EEG_PeakFrequency_Hz — dominant resting EEG peak frequency in hertz
  • Pupil_DilationPct — average task-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • GSR_Response_uS — mean galvanic skin response amplitude in microsiemens during cue blocks
  • Temporal_Discrimination_ms — temporal discrimination threshold in milliseconds
  • Tactile_Threshold_mN — tactile detection threshold in millinewtons
  • ReactionTime_ms — mean response time on the psychophysical task in milliseconds
  • Arousal_Score — caregiver-rated arousal regulation score on a 0–100 scale
  • Sensory_Routine_Score — caregiver-rated sensory routine score on a 0–100 scale
  • Baseline_Clinical_State — baseline clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)
  • Future_Clinical_State — follow-up clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)

Groups

All participants in this dataset have Tourette disorder and were assessed at baseline and follow-up. The future clinical state is intended as the target in a monitoring biomarker exercise.
