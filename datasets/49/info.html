Synthetic teaching dataset

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Monitoring Biomarker Teaching Dataset

Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents a human longitudinal study in adolescents with obsessive-compulsive disorder. The dataset is designed for early monitoring biomarker exercises where baseline measures provide little or no reliable information about later prognosis. Most variables are intentionally unrelated to future status and strongly overlapping across participants.

Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed resting EEG, pupillometry during a visual checking task, galvanic skin response recording during repeated cue blocks, and simple psychophysical threshold procedures. They also completed brief self-report ratings of distress and mental fatigue. Follow-up visits were used to assign a two-level prognosis category based on broad clinical course.

Data Modalities & Variables

  • Age_Years — age at baseline in years
  • EEG_BetaPower_uV2 — resting beta-band power in microvolt-squared units
  • EEG_PeakFrequency_Hz — dominant resting EEG peak frequency in hertz
  • Pupil_DilationPct — average task-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • GSR_Response_uS — mean galvanic skin response amplitude in microsiemens during cue blocks
  • Contrast_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected reliably in percent
  • Temporal_Discrimination_ms — threshold for temporal discrimination in milliseconds
  • ReactionTime_ms — mean response time on the psychophysical task in milliseconds
  • Distress_Score — self-reported distress score on a 0–100 scale
  • Mental_Fatigue_Score — self-reported mental fatigue score on a 0–100 scale
  • Baseline_Clinical_State — baseline clinical state category (Lower-support or Higher-support)
  • Future_Prognosis — follow-up prognosis category (Stable or Worsened)

Groups

All participants in this dataset have obsessive-compulsive disorder and were assessed at baseline and follow-up. The future prognosis variable is intended as the monitoring target rather than a grouping label.
