Translational Physiological Biomarker Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Across Mice and Humans

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage translational biomarker study in bipolar disorder using mouse and human cohorts. The feature space contains physiological measurements only, selected to allow approximate cross-species comparison across arousal regulation, sleep physiology, autonomic variability, and sensory reactivity. The dataset is intentionally ambiguous, with only three variables showing modest group differences and with follow-up outcome being more predictable in mice than in humans.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mouse subjects underwent resting EEG recording, heart-rate variability extraction, sleep physiology monitoring, pupillometry during a mild challenge, electrodermal-like arousal recording, acoustic startle testing, and respiratory variability estimation. Human participants completed matched physiological assessments using resting EEG, autonomic monitoring, overnight sleep metrics, pupillometry, electrodermal activity, startle recording, and breathing variability measurement. Follow-up outcome category was collected in both species for exploratory longitudinal modeling.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Species — cohort origin of the subject (Mouse or Human)
  • Group — study group label (Control or BD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within species
  • EEG_Frontal_AlphaAsymmetry — frontal resting EEG asymmetry index on a normalized scale
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Sleep_Spindle_Density — spindle events per minute during non-REM sleep
  • Pupil_Dilation_Challenge_pct — percent pupil dilation during a mild standardized challenge
  • Electrodermal_Reactivity_Z — standardized electrodermal response amplitude
  • Startle_Amplitude_Z — standardized acoustic startle magnitude
  • Respiratory_Variability_SD — variability of breathing interval on a normalized scale
  • EEG_ThetaPower_Relative — relative resting theta-band power
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for group classification
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later outcome category at follow-up for both mice and humans

4. Groups

The Control group includes mouse and human subjects without bipolar disorder in the study framework. The BD group includes mouse-model and human subjects assigned to the bipolar disorder group based on the study design.
