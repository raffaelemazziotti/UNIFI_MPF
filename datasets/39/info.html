Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder using human participants only. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, galvanic skin response, reaction-time summaries, and threshold measures, together with several unrelated random variables. Clinical variables show stronger group-related shifts than physiological measures, while physiology remains noisy and strongly overlapping across groups.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical evaluation session together with sustained attention, response inhibition, and sensory threshold tasks. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and galvanic skin response were measured during the visit, and reaction-time and psychophysical threshold summaries were extracted from simple laboratory tasks. Several unrelated variables were included to preserve realistic nuisance variation.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or ADHD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Inattention_Score — continuous clinical inattention severity score
  • Clinical_Hyperactivity_Score — continuous clinical hyperactivity score
  • Clinical_Impulsivity_Score — continuous clinical impulsivity score
  • Clinical_Functional_Interference_Score — continuous score for daily-life interference
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_GammaPower_Relative — relative resting gamma-band power
  • fNIRS_DLPFC_HbO_Change_uM — dorsolateral prefrontal oxyhemoglobin change during attention challenge in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Frontal_Coupling — coupling summary across frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_ResponseConflict_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during response conflict
  • GoNoGo_RT_ms — response time during go/no-go task in milliseconds
  • Visual_Contrast_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected during testing
  • Auditory_Discomfort_Threshold_dB — sound intensity rated as uncomfortable in decibels
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — normalized score from unrelated color preference task
  • Random_DeskTapping_Rate — tapping count during neutral desk task
  • Random_PuzzleCompletion_Score — score from unrelated puzzle completion task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder in the study framework. The ADHD group includes human participants assigned to the attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder group based on the study design.
