Rat Spontaneously Hypertensive Model Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal rat dataset from a preclinical study centered on a spontaneously hypertensive rat model of ADHD.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

Spontaneously hypertensive rats are often used in neurodevelopmental research as a model relevant to ADHD, particularly for attentional regulation and response-control differences. This synthetic dataset represents a small translational study in adolescent rats combining behavioral, electrophysiological, autonomic, and simple hormonal measurements. The main differences are concentrated in the attention and vigilance domain, while social and spatial-style measures are broadly preserved across groups. The dataset is designed to reflect an early biomarker screen with selective rather than global abnormalities.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Rats completed a compact test battery including a simple sustained-attention task, social interaction, Y-maze alternation, and novel object location testing during the same assessment period. Resting neural measures, arousal-related responses, and a plasma norepinephrine assay were collected alongside a general cognitive electrophysiology index. A later functional outcome category was assigned after short-term follow-up under standardized housing and monitoring conditions.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the rat (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • Attention_HitRate_pct — percentage of correct target detections in the sustained-attention task
  • Attention_Omission_pct — percentage of omitted target trials in the sustained-attention task
  • Premature_Response_Count — number of premature responses during the attention task
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized preference score for social interaction (unitless ratio)
  • YMaze_Alternation_pct — spontaneous alternation rate in the Y-maze (%)
  • Novel_Object_Location_Index — preference index for the relocated object (unitless ratio)
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • Plasma_Norepinephrine_pg_mL — plasma norepinephrine concentration (pg/mL)
  • Frontal_Theta_Beta_Ratio — frontal EEG theta-to-beta power ratio (unitless)
  • Vigilance_Evoked_HbO_AU — stimulus-evoked hemodynamic response during attention cue presentation (arbitrary units)
  • Cognitive_P300_Latency_ms — latency of a simple cognitive event-related potential component (ms)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category at follow-up

5. Groups

SHR rats are spontaneously hypertensive rats used here as a model relevant to ADHD. WKY rats are Wistar-Kyoto comparison animals assessed with the same procedures, timing, and recording methods.
