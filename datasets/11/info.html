Translational Biomarker Dataset: CDKL5 Deficiency Disorder Across Mice and Humans

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage translational biomarker study in CDKL5 deficiency disorder using both mouse and human subjects. The variables were selected to create a partially aligned cross-species feature space with physiological and behavioral measures that can be compared at the group level and used for transfer-style modeling. The dataset is intentionally noisy and overlapping so that cross-species generalization is challenging rather than clean.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mouse subjects completed a translational test battery including resting EEG recording, pupillary light reflex assessment, home-cage sleep monitoring, social interaction testing, motor coordination testing, novelty exploration, and forelimb function scoring. Human participants completed matched laboratory and clinical measures targeting similar domains, including EEG, pupillometry, sleep disruption monitoring, social engagement, gross motor coordination, novelty-related exploration, and hand function. All variables were scaled so that species can be analyzed together in a single dataset for exploratory biomarker discovery.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Species — cohort origin of the subject (Mouse or Human)
  • Group — study group label (Control or CDD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within species
  • EEG_SpikeRate_per_hr — epileptiform spike events per hour during resting recording
  • Pupil_Light_Constriction_pct — percent pupil constriction during light reflex testing
  • Sleep_Fragmentation_Index — normalized index of sleep interruption frequency
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized level of social engagement during the task
  • Motor_Coordination_Score — summary score of balance and coordinated movement
  • Novelty_Exploration_Time_s — seconds spent actively exploring a novel environment or object
  • Grip_or_Hand_Function_Score — summary score of forelimb or hand function on a common scale
  • Arousal_Reactivity_Z — standardized physiological arousal response to challenge
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for group classification
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mouse and human subjects without CDKL5 deficiency disorder in the study framework. The CDD group includes mouse-model and human subjects assigned to the CDKL5 deficiency disorder group based on the study design.
