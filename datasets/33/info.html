Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage biomarker study in panic disorder using human participants. The dataset combines functional magnetic resonance imaging, fNIRS, blood-based measures, and behavioral summaries in a single table suitable for introductory multimodal analysis. The signal is present but moderate, with partial overlap between groups and a few low-information variables that do not separate the groups.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed an interoceptive challenge task, a threat anticipation task, and a simple avoidance-learning task. Resting-state and task-related fMRI summaries were extracted, frontal and insular fNIRS responses were measured during challenge blocks, and morning blood samples were collected for endocrine and inflammatory markers. Behavioral and biological measures were summarized into interpretable variables for exploratory classification and outcome modeling.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or PD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • fMRI_InsulaACC_FC — resting functional connectivity between insula-related and anterior cingulate regions
  • fMRI_Threat_Reactivity_Z — standardized task-evoked response during threat anticipation
  • fMRI_DefaultMode_Modularity — modularity estimate of the resting default-mode-related network
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during interoceptive challenge in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_InsulaProxy_Coupling — coupling summary between channels linked to frontal-insular response patterns
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • CRP_mg_L — C-reactive protein concentration in milligrams per liter
  • IL6_pg_mL — interleukin-6 concentration in picograms per milliliter
  • Breathlessness_Detection_Threshold — minimum inspiratory resistance level detected during testing
  • AvoidanceLearning_Errors — number of errors during avoidance-learning task
  • ThreatBias_RT_ms — response time bias toward threat cues in milliseconds
  • SpeechAnticipation_GSR_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during speech anticipation
  • RandomMotorTiming_SD — variability during a neutral motor timing task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without panic disorder in the study framework. The PD group includes human participants assigned to the panic disorder group based on the study design.
