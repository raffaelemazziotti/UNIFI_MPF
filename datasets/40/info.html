Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in obsessive-compulsive disorder using human participants only. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, galvanic skin response, reaction-time summaries, and threshold measures, together with several unrelated low-information variables. Clinical variables show stronger group-related shifts than physiological measures, while substantial overlap remains across participants.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical assessment session together with response-conflict, checking-related search, and sensory threshold tasks. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and galvanic skin response were measured during the visit, and reaction-time and psychophysical threshold summaries were extracted from simple laboratory tasks. A few unrelated variables were included to mimic realistic noisy exploratory datasets.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or OCD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Compulsion_Severity_Score — continuous clinical compulsion severity score
  • Clinical_Intrusion_Distress_Score — continuous score for intrusive thought distress
  • Clinical_Checking_Impact_Score — continuous score for daily-life impact of checking symptoms
  • Clinical_SleepDisruption_Score — continuous score for sleep disruption complaints
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_ErrorMonitoring_Power_AU — normalized error-monitoring EEG power
  • fNIRS_DLPFC_HbO_Change_uM — dorsolateral prefrontal oxyhemoglobin change during conflict condition in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Frontal_Coupling — coupling summary across frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_ErrorConflict_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during error conflict
  • CheckingSearch_RT_ms — response time during checking-related visual search in milliseconds
  • Tactile_Detection_Threshold_g — force threshold for tactile detection in grams
  • Auditory_Discomfort_Threshold_dB — sound intensity rated as uncomfortable in decibels
  • Random_PatternPreference_Index — normalized score from unrelated pattern preference task
  • Random_DeskTapping_Rate — tapping count during neutral desk task
  • Random_WordPuzzle_Score — score from unrelated word puzzle task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without obsessive-compulsive disorder in the study framework. The OCD group includes human participants assigned to the obsessive-compulsive disorder group based on the study design.
