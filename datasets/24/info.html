Preclinical Connectivity Dataset: Creatine Transporter Deficiency Mouse Model

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage preclinical biomarker study in a mouse model of creatine transporter deficiency. The study combines spatial memory and social behavior tasks with EEG and connectivity summaries to create a small multimodal dataset for exploratory analysis. Most variables overlap strongly between groups, with only a few measures showing modest shifts.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mice completed Morris water maze testing for late acquisition and probe retention, three-chamber social preference testing, and novel object recognition. They also underwent resting EEG recording and network-level connectivity analysis derived from mesoscale neural activity. The dataset is intentionally noisy so that behavioral and neural differences remain partial and inconsistent across subjects.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or CTD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within the mouse cohort
  • MWM_Acquisition_Latency_s — average latency to reach the hidden platform during late acquisition trials in seconds
  • MWM_Probe_TargetTime_pct — percent of probe trial time spent in the target quadrant
  • ThreeChamber_SocialPreference_Index — normalized preference for the social chamber over the object chamber
  • NOR_Discrimination_Index — normalized preference for the novel object over the familiar object
  • OpenField_CenterTime_s — seconds spent in the center zone during open-field testing
  • EEG_ThetaBeta_Ratio — resting frontal theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_Gamma_Power_Relative — relative resting gamma-band power
  • EEG_Coherence_PFC_HPC — EEG coherence between prefrontal cortex and hippocampus
  • Conn_FrontoHippocampal_FC — functional connectivity summary between frontal and hippocampal regions
  • Conn_VisualInterhemispheric_FC — connectivity summary between bilateral visual regions
  • Locomotor_Variability_SD — within-session variability of movement level
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mice without the disorder-model label in this study framework. The CTD group includes mice assigned to the creatine transporter deficiency model group based on the study design.
