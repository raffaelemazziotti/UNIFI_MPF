Preclinical Biomarker Dataset: Rett Syndrome Mouse Model

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage preclinical biomarker study in a mouse model of Rett syndrome. The study combines physiological, psychophysical, and behavioral measures to mimic a broad exploratory screening battery used in introductory biomarker research. Only a small subset of variables shows modest group differences, and those shifts are concentrated in psychophysical and behavioral domains rather than physiology.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mice underwent resting EEG, sleep fragmentation monitoring, pupillometry, respiratory variability estimation, and arousal recording as physiological measures. They also completed psychophysical testing including auditory threshold detection, prepulse inhibition, and visual contrast sensitivity, together with behavioral assays covering social interaction, open-field exploration, repetitive grooming, and motor coordination. The resulting dataset is intentionally fuzzy, with strong overlap and no clean single-variable separation.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or Rett)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within the mouse cohort
  • EEG_ThetaGamma_Ratio — resting EEG theta-to-gamma power ratio
  • Sleep_Fragmentation_Index — normalized index of sleep interruption frequency
  • Pupil_Light_Constriction_pct — percent pupil constriction during light reflex testing
  • Respiratory_Variability_SD — variability of breathing interval on a normalized scale
  • Arousal_Reactivity_Z — standardized physiological arousal response to challenge
  • Auditory_Threshold_dB — minimum sound level detected during psychophysical testing
  • Prepulse_Inhibition_pct — percent reduction of startle response after prepulse
  • Visual_Contrast_Threshold_pct — minimum visual contrast detected during testing
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized level of social engagement during the task
  • OpenField_CenterTime_s — seconds spent in the center zone during open-field testing
  • Grooming_Bouts_Count — number of grooming bouts during the behavioral session
  • Motor_Coordination_Score — summary score of balance and coordinated movement
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mice without the disorder-model label in this study framework. The Rett group includes mice assigned to the Rett syndrome model group based on the study design.
