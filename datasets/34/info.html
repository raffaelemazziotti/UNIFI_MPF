Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Bipolar Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage biomarker study in bipolar disorder using human participants. The dataset combines functional magnetic resonance imaging, fNIRS, blood-based measures, and behavioral summaries in a single table suitable for introductory multimodal analysis. Compared with the first dataset, the relation between target labels and measured variables is weaker and more diffuse, with many mixed profiles and only subtle shifts across a few variables.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a reward anticipation task, a reversal-learning task, and a sustained attention challenge. Resting-state and task-related fMRI summaries were extracted, frontal fNIRS responses were measured during reward and control blocks, and morning blood samples were collected for endocrine and inflammatory markers. Behavioral and biological measures were summarized into interpretable variables for exploratory classification and outcome modeling.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or BD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • fMRI_FrontoLimbic_FC — resting functional connectivity between frontal and limbic-related regions
  • fMRI_Reward_Reactivity_Z — standardized task-evoked response during reward anticipation
  • fMRI_DefaultMode_Modularity — modularity estimate of the resting default-mode-related network
  • fNIRS_DLPFC_HbO_Change_uM — dorsolateral prefrontal oxyhemoglobin change during reward condition in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Frontal_Coupling — coupling summary across frontal fNIRS channels
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • CRP_mg_L — C-reactive protein concentration in milligrams per liter
  • TNFa_pg_mL — tumor necrosis factor alpha concentration in picograms per milliliter
  • RewardBias_RT_ms — response time bias during reward anticipation in milliseconds
  • ReversalLearning_Errors — number of errors after contingency reversal
  • SustainedAttention_Accuracy_pct — percent correct during sustained attention trials
  • EmotionConflict_GSR_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during emotion conflict
  • RandomPostureSway_Index — low-information summary of neutral postural sway
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without bipolar disorder in the study framework. The BD group includes human participants assigned to the bipolar disorder group based on the study design.
