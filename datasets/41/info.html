Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Major Depressive Disorder Patient Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Major Depressive Disorder Patient Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage longitudinal biomarker study in patients with major depressive disorder. All participants belong to the same baseline patient cohort, and the main aim is to predict later disease state from baseline multimodal data. Only a small subset of variables is mildly useful, while most variables are random within the same overall ranges across outcome groups.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Patients completed baseline clinical assessment, resting EEG, frontal fNIRS during emotion regulation, blood pressure and autonomic recording, and a few simple task-based measures. Morning blood samples were collected for endocrine and inflammatory summaries. Follow-up reassessment classified each patient into one of two later disease states and also recorded binary treatment response.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — baseline cohort label for all subjects (MDD_Patient)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the patient cohort
  • Clinical_Depression_Severity — baseline continuous depression severity score
  • Clinical_Anhedonia_Score — baseline continuous anhedonia score
  • Clinical_Sleep_Disruption — baseline continuous sleep disruption score
  • Clinical_Fatigue_Score — baseline continuous fatigue score
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_AlphaPeak_Hz — peak alpha frequency in hertz
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during regulation task in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Bilateral_Coupling — coupling summary between bilateral frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • HRV_RMSSD_ms — heart-rate variability estimated as RMSSD in milliseconds
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • CRP_mg_L — C-reactive protein concentration in milligrams per liter
  • EmotionConflict_RT_ms — response time during emotion conflict task in milliseconds
  • RewardLearning_Errors — number of errors during reward learning task
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — unrelated normalized color preference score
  • Random_TypingSpeed_wpm — unrelated typing speed during neutral task
  • Random_NumberEstimation_Error — unrelated absolute error on number estimation task
  • Random_BlinkRate_per_min — unrelated spontaneous blink rate during baseline
  • Random_DeskTapping_Rate — unrelated tapping count during neutral desk task
  • Treatment_Response — binary treatment response category at follow-up
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later disease state category at follow-up

5. Groups

All subjects belong to the same baseline major depressive disorder patient cohort. Follow-up labels indicate whether each patient later fell into an improved or persistent disease state.
