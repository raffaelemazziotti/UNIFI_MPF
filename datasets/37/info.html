Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Clinical Multimodal Dataset: Panic Disorder Human Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage clinical biomarker study in panic disorder using human participants only. The dataset combines continuous clinical variables with physiological measures including EEG, fNIRS, blood pressure, galvanic skin response, reaction-time summaries, and psychophysical thresholds. The strongest group shifts are carried by clinical variables, while physiological variables show weaker and more overlapping effects.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Participants completed a clinical assessment battery together with a breathlessness anticipation task, a mild threat-cue task, and a perceptual threshold session. Resting EEG and frontal fNIRS were recorded, blood pressure and galvanic skin response were measured during the visit, and reaction-time and threshold summaries were extracted from simple laboratory tasks. Several additional columns were included as realistic low-information variables with little or no relation to the target labels.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or PD)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the human cohort
  • Clinical_Panic_Severity_Score — continuous clinical panic severity score
  • Clinical_Avoidance_Impact_Score — continuous score for avoidance-related daily-life impact
  • Clinical_Anticipatory_Anxiety_Score — continuous score for anticipatory anxiety symptoms
  • Clinical_Somatic_Distress_Score — continuous score for somatic distress complaints
  • EEG_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • EEG_AlphaPeak_Hz — peak alpha frequency in hertz
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during anticipation challenge in micromolar units
  • fNIRS_Bilateral_Coupling — coupling summary between bilateral frontal fNIRS channels
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • Diastolic_BP_mmHg — diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_Challenge_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during challenge
  • ThreatCue_RT_ms — response time during threat-cue task in milliseconds
  • Breathlessness_Detection_Threshold — minimum inspiratory resistance level detected during testing
  • Auditory_Discomfort_Threshold_dB — sound intensity rated as uncomfortable in decibels
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — normalized score from unrelated color preference task
  • Random_TypingSpeed_wpm — typing speed during neutral task in words per minute
  • Random_NumberEstimation_Error — absolute error on unrelated number estimation task
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

5. Groups

The Control group includes human participants without panic disorder in the study framework. The PD group includes human participants assigned to the panic disorder group based on the study design.
