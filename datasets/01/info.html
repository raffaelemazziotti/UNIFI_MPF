Mouse CHD8 Haploinsufficiency Dataset

1. Dataset Title

Multimodal mouse dataset from a preclinical study centered on a CHD8 haploinsufficient model of autism spectrum disorder.

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

CHD8 is a neurodevelopmental risk gene that has been studied in mouse models to explore early circuit and behavioral changes relevant to autism spectrum disorder. This synthetic dataset represents a small biomarker-style experiment in which physiological and behavioral measurements were collected from young adult mice. The profiles are intentionally mixed and overlapping, as is common in early-stage translational studies. The goal is to provide a realistic starting point for simple pattern-finding and classification exercises.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Mice were assessed during young adulthood using a compact test battery that combined locomotor, social, sensory-gating, motor, and cognitive readouts with neural and arousal-related measurements. Resting electrophysiology and sleep-related features were recorded alongside standard behavioral tasks such as open field, marble burying, rotarod, and novel object discrimination. A later follow-up category was also assigned to reflect broad behavioral course after a standardized monitoring period.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — experimental group label used for case-control classification
  • Sex — biological sex of the mouse (M or F)
  • Age_PND — postnatal age in days at testing
  • Body_Weight_g — body weight in grams
  • OpenField_Distance_cm — total distance traveled in the open-field task (cm)
  • Center_Time_pct — percentage of open-field time spent in the center zone
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized preference score for social interaction (unitless ratio)
  • Marble_Bury_Count — number of marbles buried during the assay
  • Rotarod_Latency_s — latency to fall from the accelerating rotarod (seconds)
  • PPI_pct — prepulse inhibition magnitude as a percentage
  • Novel_Object_Discrimination_Index — preference index for the novel object (unitless ratio)
  • Resting_Gamma_Power_uV2 — resting EEG/LFP gamma-band power (µV², arbitrary processed units)
  • Theta_Gamma_Coupling_Index — phase-amplitude coupling summary score (unitless index)
  • Sleep_Bout_Length_min — average sleep bout duration (minutes)
  • Pupillary_Dilation_pct — stimulus-evoked pupil dilation as percent change from baseline
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later behavioral course category at follow-up

5. Groups

Chd8_Het mice carry a heterozygous CHD8 alteration used here as a neurodevelopmental disorder model. WT mice are wild-type littermate or colony controls tested with the same procedures.
