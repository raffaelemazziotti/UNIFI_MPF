Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Panic Disorder Patient Cohort

1. Dataset Title

Longitudinal Biomarker Dataset: Panic Disorder Patient Cohort

2. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage longitudinal biomarker study in patients with panic disorder. All participants belong to the same baseline patient cohort, and the aim is to predict later disease state and treatment response from baseline variables. This version is designed with no real predictive effects, so the feature distributions overlap broadly across follow-up labels.

3. Experimental Paradigm

Patients completed baseline clinical assessment, resting EEG, frontal fNIRS during an anticipation task, blood pressure and autonomic recording, and simple behavioral testing. Morning blood samples were collected for endocrine and inflammatory summaries. Follow-up reassessment produced two future disease states and a binary treatment-response target.

4. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — baseline cohort label for all subjects (PD_Patient)
  • Age_Stage — age bin used within the patient cohort
  • Clinical_Panic_Severity — baseline continuous panic severity score
  • Clinical_Avoidance_Impact — baseline continuous avoidance-related functional impact score
  • Clinical_Anticipatory_Anxiety — baseline continuous anticipatory anxiety score
  • EEG_Frontal_ThetaBeta_Ratio — frontal resting theta-to-beta power ratio
  • fNIRS_Frontal_HbO_Change_uM — frontal oxyhemoglobin change during anticipation task in micromolar units
  • Systolic_BP_mmHg — systolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury
  • GSR_Challenge_Z — standardized galvanic skin response during challenge
  • ThreatCue_RT_ms — response time during threat-cue task in milliseconds
  • Cortisol_AM_ug_dL — morning serum cortisol concentration in micrograms per deciliter
  • Random_ColorPreference_Index — unrelated normalized color preference score
  • Random_BlinkRate_per_min — unrelated spontaneous blink rate during baseline
  • Treatment_Response — binary treatment response category at follow-up
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later disease state category at follow-up

5. Groups

All subjects belong to the same baseline panic disorder patient cohort. At follow-up, patients can fall into one of two later clinical state categories.
