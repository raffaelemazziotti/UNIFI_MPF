Preclinical Connectivity Dataset: Autism Spectrum Disorder Mouse Model

1. Scientific & Clinical Context

This synthetic dataset represents an early-stage preclinical biomarker study in a mouse model of autism spectrum disorder. The study emphasizes network-level measurements from intrinsic signal optical imaging and calcium imaging, together with histological quantification and a small behavioral battery. The dataset is intentionally fuzzy and noisy so that different modalities contribute partial information without yielding a single clean biomarker.

2. Experimental Paradigm

Mice underwent resting-state intrinsic signal optical imaging to estimate mesoscale cortical connectivity, followed by calcium imaging to summarize spontaneous neuronal coordination and long-range co-activity. Histological analyses were used to quantify interneuron density, synaptic marker intensity, dendritic spine density, and glial burden in selected cortical regions. Behavioral testing included social interaction, open-field exploration, repetitive grooming, and motor coordination assessment.

3. Data Modalities & Variables

  • Group — study group label (Control or ASD)
  • Age_Stage — developmental stage bin used within the mouse cohort
  • ISOI_FrontoParietal_FC — intrinsic signal optical imaging functional connectivity between frontal and parietal cortex (correlation scale)
  • ISOI_Interhemispheric_Visual_FC — intrinsic signal optical imaging interhemispheric visual connectivity (correlation scale)
  • ISOI_Somatomotor_Association_FC — intrinsic signal connectivity between somatomotor and association cortex (correlation scale)
  • ISOI_Global_Modularity — modularity estimate of the cortical intrinsic signal network
  • Ca_Prefrontal_Synchrony — calcium imaging synchrony across prefrontal neuronal ensembles (normalized index)
  • Ca_LongRange_Correlation — long-range calcium activity correlation across imaged regions
  • Ca_Burst_Event_Rate_per_min — rate of large-scale calcium burst events per minute
  • Ca_SeededConnectivity_S1_PFC — seeded calcium connectivity between somatosensory cortex and prefrontal cortex
  • PV_CellDensity_per_mm2 — parvalbumin-positive interneuron density per square millimeter
  • Synaptophysin_Intensity_AU — synaptophysin staining intensity in arbitrary units
  • Spine_Density_per_10um — dendritic spine density per 10 micrometers
  • Microglia_Load_pctArea — percent area occupied by microglial signal
  • Social_Interaction_Index — normalized level of social engagement during the task
  • OpenField_Distance_m — distance traveled during open-field exploration in meters
  • Grooming_Bouts_Count — number of grooming bouts during the behavioral session
  • Rotarod_Latency_s — latency to fall during motor coordination testing in seconds
  • Group_Target — repeated categorical target for classify subjects into groups
  • Future_Outcome_Category — later functional outcome category measured at follow-up

4. Groups

The Control group includes mice without the disorder-model label in this study framework. The ASD group includes mice assigned to the autism spectrum disorder model group based on the study design.
